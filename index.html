Plaintext · Typography as art

Typographic wallpaper generator

Plaintext turns a quote into a minimal typographic poster. Pick a quote from 200+ curated lines, choose a typeface, a layout and a background, then download a high-resolution wallpaper for desktop, mobile, Instagram or social previews.

What you can make

  • Desktop wallpapers

    1920 × 1080, 2K, 4K and ultrawide exports, rendered at native resolution.

  • Phone wallpapers

    1080 × 1920 portraits with tighter leading and margins adapted to the format.

  • Instagram posts

    Square 1080 × 1080 compositions with room for the quote to breathe.

  • OG & Twitter cards

    1200 × 630 previews for articles, newsletters and link previews.

  • Three layouts

    Editorial, Ruled and Offset, all built on a Swiss grid.

  • Ten typefaces

    Curated Google Fonts, plus solid, gradient and typographic-pattern backgrounds.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick a quote

    Browse the library by vibe (calm, serious or chaotic) or type your own text.

  2. 02

    Style it

    Choose typeface, layout, background and ink. Auto-contrast picks a readable ink for you.

  3. 03

    Download

    Export a PNG at the exact resolution you need. No account, no watermark.

About Plaintext

Plaintext is a free, single-purpose tool: it makes typographic posters out of words. It was built for people who like type, and it follows the idea that less is more. No templates, no stickers, no effects.

Everything renders in your browser on a canvas. Your text never leaves your device, there is nothing to sign up for and the exports carry no watermark.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plaintext free?

Yes. The generator, the quote library and every export are free, with no account and no watermark.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser and works on desktop and mobile.

Can I use the wallpapers commercially?

The quotes are in the public domain or short attributions; the typography comes from Google Fonts under open licences. The generated images are yours to use.

Does Plaintext store my text?

No. Rendering happens locally on a canvas, so your text is never sent to a server.

Which resolution should I choose?

1920 × 1080 for most screens, 4K for high-density displays, 1080 × 1920 for phones and 1080 × 1080 for Instagram.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. The interface and the quote library are bilingual, and there is a Spanish version of the site.
